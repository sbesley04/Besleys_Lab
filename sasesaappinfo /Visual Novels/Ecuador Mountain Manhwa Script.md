# ECUADOR MOUNTAIN FAN FIC
## Episode 1: The Last Can

**Format:** Vertical-scroll manhwa episode  
**Tone:** Warm ensemble comedy; cinematic mountain atmosphere interrupted by petty catastrophe.  
**Reading direction:** Let wide landscape panels breathe. Use quick reaction cuts and isolated dialogue bubbles for the comedy.

---

### OPENING — THE MOUNTAIN DOES NOT CARE

**PANEL 1 — FULL-WIDTH ESTABLISHING**

An Ecuadorian mountain range at blue hour. A tiny campsite clings to a high rocky shelf. The sky is enormous, layered with cold clouds and the first stars.

**CAPTION:**
Night three was when everything fell apart.

**PANEL 2 — TALL, NARROW**

Close on frost glittering along a tent rope. Wind bends the grass.

**CAPTION:**
Not because of the altitude.

**PANEL 3 — TALL, NARROW**

William, bundled up and grinning, is halfway through explaining something with both hands. Colin sits across from him, politely trapped in the conversation.

**CAPTION:**
Not because of the cold.

**WILLIAM:**
I’m telling you, the unfinished degree is more of a philosophical issue than an academic one.

**PANEL 4 — TALL, NARROW**

Gabe kneels beside an open hiking pack. The tiny cooler compartment is unmistakably empty.

**CAPTION:**
And not because William was technically not actually post-grad.

**PANEL 5 — CLOSE-UP**

Gabe’s expression goes utterly blank.

**GABE:**
No.

**PANEL 6 — WIDE SILENT BEAT**

William and Colin look over. Wind tears through the dark campsite.

**WILLIAM:**
What?

**PANEL 7 — CLOSE-UP**

Gabe holds up the final empty can between two fingers, like evidence at a trial.

**GABE:**
We have no beer.

**PANEL 8 — EXTRA-WIDE**

The three of them, tiny beneath the mountain and sky. No one moves.

**SFX:** WHOOOOOSH

**PANEL 9 — SMALL INSERT**

A distant bird takes off from a rock.

**SFX:** KRRR.

**CAPTION:**
Somewhere in the darkness, a bird made a noise that sounded vaguely disappointed.

**PANEL 10 — CLOSE ON WILLIAM**

His smile has vanished.

**WILLIAM:**
That’s impossible.

**PANEL 11 — THREE SMALL REACTION PANELS**

Gabe stares at the can. William stares at Gabe. Colin stares at both of them, already sensing danger.

**CAPTION:**
The realization settled over the campsite like a funeral shroud.

**WILLIAM:**
Oh.

---

### THE TRIAL OF THE MISSING BEER

**PANEL 12 — VERTICAL ACTION BEAT**

William rises to his feet. Gabe rises at exactly the same time. Colin remains seated on a rock with his trail mix.

**GABE:**
This is your fault.

**WILLIAM:**
My fault?

**PANEL 13 — MEDIUM ON GABE**

Gabe points toward the single battered suitcase beside the tent.

**GABE:**
You argued for one suitcase.

**PANEL 14 — MEDIUM ON WILLIAM**

William points back, offended on principle.

**WILLIAM:**
Because carrying two suitcases up a mountain is insane.

**PANEL 15 — TWO-PANEL QUICK CUT**

**GABE:**
It would’ve been worth it.

**WILLIAM:**
For beer?

**PANEL 16 — CLOSE ON GABE, DEAD SERIOUS**

**GABE:**
For enough beer.

**PANEL 17 — WIDE**

Their argument gains speed. Their silhouettes throw long fire-lit shadows. Colin casually eats trail mix in the foreground.

**GABE:**
You wanted to bring forty-eight cans.

**WILLIAM:**
We *did* bring forty-eight cans.

**GABE:**
And somehow you’re acting like we planned poorly.

**WILLIAM:**
Because we did.

**PANEL 18 — CLOSE ON COLIN**

He tries to lift one finger between them.

**COLIN:**
Maybe we could—

**PANEL 19 — SAME COMPOSITION, LARGER BUBBLES FROM OFF-PANEL**

Gabe and William’s argument steamrolls straight past him.

**GABE (OFF):**
You don’t understand logistics.

**WILLIAM (OFF):**
You don’t understand basic physics!

**PANEL 20 — TINY COLIN REACTION**

Colin lowers his hand. He looks back at the trail mix.

**COLIN (THOUGHT):**
Neptune would’ve appreciated me.

**CAPTION:**
Colin was beginning to suspect he’d been invited solely to prevent the appearance of a honeymoon.

---

### FORTY MINUTES OF DARKNESS

**PANEL 21 — LONG VERTICAL PANEL**

The campfire has burned low. Gabe sits at the far left, dramatically staring into one side of the night. William sits at the far right, dramatically staring into another. Colin is stranded between them on a rock.

**CAPTION:**
An hour later, neither was speaking.

**PANEL 22 — SPLIT REACTION**

Gabe: arms folded, jaw set.  
William: arms folded, jaw set.

**CAPTION:**
Neither was looking at the other.

**PANEL 23 — WIDE**

Colin is perfectly centered between the two. The empty space around him is almost absurd.

**COLIN:**
I feel like I’m the child in a divorce.

**PANEL 24 — SILENT NARROW PANEL**

No reaction from either side.

**PANEL 25 — CLOSE ON COLIN**

**COLIN:**
I miss Neptune.

**PANEL 26 — EVEN SMALLER SILENT PANEL**

A campfire ember pops.

**SFX:** TCK.

**CAPTION:**
Still nothing.

**PANEL 27 — NIGHT SKY TRANSITION**

Stars wheel above the peak.

**CAPTION:**
The silence lasted another forty minutes.

---

### THE COUGH

**PANEL 28 — CLOSE ON GABE**

Gabe turns away and coughs once into his fist.

**SFX:** COUGH.

**PANEL 29 — SHARP CLOSE ON WILLIAM**

His whole posture breaks. Concern arrives before he can hide it.

**WILLIAM:**
You okay?

**PANEL 30 — WIDE, QUIET**

Gabe looks across the fire. William is already looking back. Colin catches the moment and slowly stops chewing.

**PANEL 31 — CLOSE ON GABE**

He crosses his arms again, but the anger has lost its edge.

**GABE:**
You started it.

**PANEL 32 — CLOSE ON WILLIAM**

**WILLIAM:**
You called me irresponsible.

**PANEL 33 — CLOSE ON GABE**

**GABE:**
You *are* irresponsible.

**PANEL 34 — CLOSE ON WILLIAM**

His expression turns incredulous.

**WILLIAM:**
You’re the one who wanted to haul sixty pounds of beer up a volcano.

**PANEL 35 — NARROW, SILENT**

Gabe opens his mouth to answer. Stops.

**PANEL 36 — CLOSE ON WILLIAM**

He tries not to smile.

**WILLIAM:**
It would’ve solved the current situation.

**PANEL 37 — BIG RELEASE PANEL**

William laughs—small at first, then helplessly. Gabe gives in at the same time. The tension dissolves into laughter under the stars.

**SFX:** HAH—

**CAPTION:**
The fight collapsed under its own absurdity.

---

### BROTHERLY AFFECTION

**PANEL 38 — WARM THREE-SHOT**

The three sit closer to the fire now. Colin watches Gabe and William like someone observing an unusual animal behavior.

**WILLIAM:**
I’m sorry.

**GABE:**
I’m sorry too.

**PANEL 39 — CLOSE ON GABE**

He points at William with mock accusation.

**GABE:**
You are still a trigger. It’s not my fault we fought. You’re just more invested than I am. And I miss—

**PANEL 40 — CLOSE ON WILLIAM**

William receives this like an old weather report.

**WILLIAM:**
Fair.

**PANEL 41 — FULL-WIDTH NIGHT PANEL**

The mountain is silent. Their little fire is a warm ember in the dark.

**PANEL 42 — CLOSE ON GABE**

After a small pause:

**GABE:**
Love you, bro.

**PANEL 43 — CLOSE ON WILLIAM**

Immediate, automatic, completely sincere.

**WILLIAM:**
Love you too, bro.

**PANEL 44 — CLOSE ON COLIN**

He slowly lowers a granola bar. His face is blank with disbelief.

**COLIN:**
Jesus Christ.

**PANEL 45 — WILLIAM, GRINNING**

**WILLIAM:**
Don’t say the Lord’s name in vain. I can’t go to church on this mountain.

**CAPTION:**
His smile was bright like a bag of diamonds.

---

### EPILOGUE — NIGHT THREE IS GOING GREAT

**PANEL 46 — DEEP-BLUE EXTERIOR**

Later. The tents glow dimly beneath a field of stars. The temperature has fallen sharply.

**CAPTION:**
Later that night, the temperature dropped sharply.

**PANEL 47 — INSIDE COLIN’S TENT**

Colin lies awake in his sleeping bag. Through the mesh overhead: stars. From the neighboring tent, Gabe and William’s muffled voices drift through the night.

**GABE (OFF):**
No, that’s not what happened.

**WILLIAM (OFF):**
That is exactly what happened!

**SFX:** HAH HA HA.

**PANEL 48 — CLOSE ON COLIN**

He looks at the empty space beside him.

**COLIN:**
You know, Neptune would’ve slept on the floor with me.

**PANEL 49 — FINAL LONG PANEL**

View from outside Colin’s tent: the mountain, the limitless stars, and the faint glow of the neighboring tent where Gabe and William are still laughing.

**CAPTION:**
The mountain offered no response.

**CAPTION:**
Night three was going great.

**END OF EPISODE 1**
