# The Last Can — generation notes

## Mode

Built-in image generation, using the three local character sheets as identity
references. The art is new generation from references, not edits to the sheets.

## Shared production prompt

- Use case: `illustration-story`
- Asset type: vertical-scroll manhwa episode illustration
- Setting: a three-person campsite on a high Ecuadorian Andean shelf at blue
  hour and night
- Style: polished modern Korean webtoon/manhwa illustration, crisp confident
  linework, expressive acting, subtle painterly cel shading, cinematic depth,
  professional published quality
- Gabe: olive-tan skin, thick short black curls, black insulated hiking layers,
  thin gold chain
- William: fair skin, tousled sandy-blond hair, pale blue-and-white insulated
  hiking jacket, dark pants
- Colin: fair skin, straight medium-brown hair, muted olive-green insulated
  hiking jacket, dark pants
- Format: portrait 2:3, designed for a phone-width vertical scroll
- Palette: cold cobalt/slate night, warm amber firelight
- Constraints: exactly three young adult men when all are present; preserve
  faces, hair, and color-coded outfits; no readable text, speech bubbles, logos,
  or watermark

## Scene prompt set

1. Enormous mountain establishing shot with the tiny campsite and first stars.
2. William explains his degree with both hands while Colin is politely trapped;
   Gabe freezes over an empty pack in the background.
3. Gabe holds the final empty can like courtroom evidence while William and
   Colin register mock-tragic catastrophe.
4. Gabe and William point at one another across the fire beside a battered
   suitcase; Colin calmly eats trail mix in the foreground.
5. Colin raises one finger to mediate while the two larger arguing profiles
   visually steamroll past him.
6. Gabe and William sit at opposite extremes of the campsite with arms folded;
   Colin is stranded in exaggerated empty space between them.
7. Gabe coughs once; William's stubborn posture breaks into immediate concern;
   Colin notices the tenderness.
8. Gabe and William give in to helpless laughter under the stars while Colin
   watches, paused mid-snack.
9. Gabe and William exchange sincere brotherly smiles while Colin slowly lowers
   a granola bar in flat disbelief.
10. From Colin's tent, he lies awake beside an empty space while two laughing
    silhouettes glow in the neighboring tent beneath the mountain sky.

All final lettering is HTML rather than generated pixels so the script remains
verbatim, selectable, accessible, and sharp at every screen size. In the
stitched edition, captions, dialogue bubbles, chapter labels, and sound effects
are positioned directly over each illustration. The ten illustrated panels
touch edge-to-edge to form one uninterrupted vertical webtoon strip.
